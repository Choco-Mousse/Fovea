Micro-Transponder Hardware Specs

Designed for zero ergonomic compromise. Weighting less than a single gram, the transponder tucks cleanly into standard titanium or acetate hinges without altering frame aesthetics.

Dual-Band UWB + BLE

Harnesses IEEE 802.15.4z Ultra-Wideband Channels 5 & 9 for millimeter Time-of-Flight ranging and BLE 5.3 Long-Range background advertising.

±5 cm Ranging Accuracy

Piezo Acoustic Cavity

Custom miniaturized 3.2mm ceramic piezoelectric resonator emits a penetrating 2.8kHz - 3.8kHz acoustic chirp audible through cushions and bags.

85 dB SPL @ 10cm

Solar & Kinetic Harvest

A micro photovoltaic strip along the upper rim coupled with a solid-state micro-battery provides up to 14 months of maintenance-free operation.

14 Months Autonomous

0.8g Micro Form Factor

Encapsulated in medical-grade biocompatible resin with IP68 waterproof rating. Featherweight construction ensures identical center of gravity.
